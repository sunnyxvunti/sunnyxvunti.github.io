03 / Working principles

How I think.

Three habits connect everything from product-data cleanup to systems simulation.

01 — Observe

Go to the source.

Watch the process, ask people doing the work, and validate the raw data. A dashboard cannot fix an inaccurate input.

02 — Test

Make assumptions visible.

Use models, experiments, and process mapping to test explanations before treating them as fact.

03 — Communicate

Build for decisions.

A useful result explains what changes, why it matters, what remains uncertain, and what to do next.
