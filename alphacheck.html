AlphaCheck / AI-assisted prototype

A financial-analysis prototype that asks whether the inputs can be trusted.

A polished valuation output means little if a source field is missing, inferred, or inconsistent. AlphaCheck explores a verification-first approach to extracting and organizing public financial information.

The problem

Financial dashboards can make uncertain inputs look authoritative. I wanted to design a tool where the provenance and completeness of a field are evaluated before a conclusion is displayed.

Prototype

AlphaCheck is an experimental browser-based financial-analysis app developed with AI-assisted coding tools. The project includes SEC EDGAR retrieval, structured financial fields, ratio calculations, source precedence, missing-data flags, and reporting views for financial statements, risks, and valuation-related analysis.

My contribution and workflow

I defined the use case and required checks, directed AI-assisted implementation, and reviewed how the interface and calculations behaved. The core product decision was to make data-quality signals visible and flag cases where the inputs are incomplete or insufficient.

Limits

This is a beta prototype, not a production investment product. Code structure and some tests do not by themselves prove completeness, model validity, live-data reliability, or the suitability of any investment decision. No personalized investment recommendation is intended.

Why it belongs in this portfolio

It demonstrates an engineering habit that applies far beyond finance: design the validation and exception-handling process before automating the final recommendation.
